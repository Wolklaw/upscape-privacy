Upscape Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Upscape is a single-player arcade game made by Wolklaw. This policy covers the Upscape app for Android, Windows (including the Steam version) and the web.

What we collect

Nothing. Upscape does not collect, use, sell or share any personal information or device data. The game has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not need an internet connection.

Google Play and Steam versions

The versions of Upscape on Google Play and Steam make no network requests at all. The Android app from Google Play does not even have the internet permission. Updates come from the store.

Direct downloads

A copy of Upscape that you get directly from the developer, rather than from Google Play or Steam, has two extra features. The main menu has a tip button that opens paypal.me/wolklaw in your browser. Nothing is sent to PayPal by the game, and tipping is handled entirely by PayPal under its own terms and privacy policy. And when your device is online, the game asks GitHub, where the developer publishes direct downloads, whether a newer version exists, so it can offer to update. This request contains no personal information; like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address. Updates are only downloaded when you tap the update button.

What stays on your device

The game saves your high scores, the name you type for the high score board, which climbers you have unlocked and your settings, such as sound and level width. This information is stored only on your device (on Windows, in the Upscape folder inside your local app data) and is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

On Android, if you use your device's backup feature, the backup may include this saved game data, the same as for any other app. That backup is handled by your device and your Google account, not by us.

Permissions

The Android app from Google Play asks for no permissions. A directly downloaded Android app uses internet access only to check for updates, and asks for no other permissions.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Wolklaw/upscape-privacy/issues.
